# `vertical()` takes two sources per family, and that is not redundancy

The blurred band comes from the *unbranded* cut, the centered frame from the
*branded* one. Both layers used to come from the branded cut, and because the band
is the source scaled ~1.8x and centre-cropped, the lower third reappeared in it
large enough to read the street address off. Do not collapse it back to a single
input. Sourcing both layers from the unbranded cut also removes the ghost — by
stripping the branding off the social cut, which is not the same fix.
